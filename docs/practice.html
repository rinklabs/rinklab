Practice Lab Quick Guide

Adding Drills

Click or drag a library drill onto the list
Use Quick Drill for non-diagram items (water break, scrimmage, talk)

Reordering

Drag the ⠿ handle to reorder drills
Edit the minutes field on any drill to adjust its time

Simultaneous Stations

on a drill links it with the next as one station block
A station block shares one duration, counted once in the total
on a block splits it back into sequential drills

Saving & Teams

Ctrl+S Save practice
Pick a team in "Team" to share it, or leave as private
Library loads a saved practice (scoped to the selected team)

Sharing & Export

Share generates a link that opens this exact plan
PDF exports a printable plan with drill diagrams

Tip

Set your Target duration at the top — the timeline bar at the bottom turns orange once your drills run over it.
