Who We Are

We're volunteer coaches who got tired of hunting for good, free planning tools. Our kids play house league — short practices, limited ice time — so every minute counts. Most tools out there gate features behind paywalls or bury you in options built for rep-level hockey. We wanted something free, simple, and built for coaches like us. So we built it.

Our Values

Built by volunteers, for volunteers. It stays free — no fees for you, your team, or your league. One goal: kids learn, improve, and keep playing.

Our Motivation

House league taught us how to support teammates, respect opponents, win with grace, lose with grace. It gave us friendships and a place to belong. The rink isn't just ice — it's community. We built this so every coach gets a solid practice-planning tool without expensive software standing in the way.

Our Core Coaching Values

We coach for effort, growth, and team — not just wins. Every drill should build better teammates, not just better players.

  • Relentless Effort: Compete for every puck. Effort over perfection.
  • Coachability: Listen with intent. Stay open to the game.
  • Team First: Have your teammates' backs, on the ice and off.
