FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Free hockey drills & practice planning for coaches
← Back to Lab

Rink Labs is a free place for volunteer hockey coaches to find drills, draw their own and plan practices. Here's what coaches ask us most.

Where can I find free hockey drills for coaching?

Rink Labs has a free drill library built by volunteer coaches. Browse the Drill Library, search by title or tag, and open any drill to view it, copy it, and edit it for your own practice. There's no paywall and no trial period.

Is Rink Labs really free?

Yes. Rink Labs is built by volunteer coaches and it stays free, with no fees for you, your team or your league. We built it because most coaching tools gate features behind paywalls or are built for rep-level hockey rather than house league.

Who is Rink Labs for?

Volunteer and amateur coaches, especially in youth and house league hockey where ice time is short and every minute counts. You don't need design or tech skills. See Our Values & Motivation for the full story.

How do I draw a hockey drill online?

Open the drill canvas, pick a full or half rink, and place players, pylons, nets and pucks. Add lines, arrows and text to show the movement, then give the drill a title and tags. It works on desktop, tablet and phone, and you can print a drill straight from the page.

How do I build a hockey practice plan?

Open the practice builder, drag drills in from the library, and set a time for each one. You can add quick items like a warm-up, set a target length such as 60 minutes, and group drills that run at the same time as stations. Then print it, export a PDF, or share a link.

Do I need an account?

You can start drawing right away. A free account is only needed to save drills and practice plans to the cloud and to use teams. Signing up takes a minute and you can delete your account at any time from Account Settings, which permanently erases your drills, plans and team memberships.

Can my assistant coaches use the same practice plans?

Yes. Create a team (or join one with its code) and save practices to it, and everyone on the team can open them. Assistant coaches need to sign in and select the team on the practice page to see the team's plans. You can also send a share link.

Are my drills and practice plans public?

Drills you save go into the shared library so other coaches can learn from them. That's the idea behind "public drills, private practices". Practice plans stay private unless you save them to a team or share a link. If you see a drill that shouldn't be in the library, use the report button.

Can I copy or edit someone else's drill?

Yes. Open any drill from the library and make it your own. Saving creates your own copy, so the original is never changed.

Can I print or export practice plans and drills?

Yes. You can print any drill from the canvas, and export a practice plan as a PDF to hand out or post at the rink.

Does Rink Labs work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. The drill canvas supports touch, including pinch to zoom and two-finger pan, and the toolbar collapses into a drawer on small screens.

Is Rink Labs open source? Can I help?

Yes. Rink Labs is open source on GitHub and we welcome help, whether that's code, drills, testing or ideas. See the contact details to get in touch.

Can I use Rink Labs for a paid program or professional team?

Rink Labs is made for volunteer and community coaching. If you'd like to use it commercially or in a professional or competitive setting, please read the Terms & Conditions and get in touch first.
